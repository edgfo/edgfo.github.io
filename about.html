Who We Are

Enterprise Development & Governance Facility

Enterprise Development & Governance Facility (EDGF) is committed to supporting individuals, enterprises, institutions and communities to develop their capacity, strengthen governance and create sustainable opportunities.


We believe that sustainable development requires a combination of strong institutions, responsible leadership, vibrant enterprises, innovation and meaningful participation by communities.

Our Direction

What guides our work

Our Mission

To empower individuals, enterprises and communities through enterprise development, capacity building, governance support, innovation and strategic partnerships that create sustainable opportunities.

Our Vision

A society where enterprises thrive, institutions are accountable and transparent, and communities have the capacity and opportunities to achieve sustainable development.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide our decisions and actions.

Integrity

We promote honesty, ethical conduct and responsible leadership.

Accountability

We take responsibility for our decisions, resources and results.

Transparency

We value openness and clear communication with our stakeholders.

Inclusion

We seek to ensure that different groups can participate and benefit from development opportunities.

Innovation

We encourage creative approaches to development challenges.

Partnership

We believe lasting development is achieved through collaboration.

Our Approach

How EDGF seeks to create meaningful and sustainable impact.

Capacity Building: We strengthen knowledge, skills and institutional capacity.
Enterprise Support: We promote entrepreneurship, innovation and sustainable enterprise development.
Good Governance: We support accountability, transparency, participation and effective institutional practices.
Community Participation: We encourage communities to participate actively in identifying and addressing their development priorities.
Partnerships: We collaborate with public institutions, private sector actors, civil society, communities and development partners.
