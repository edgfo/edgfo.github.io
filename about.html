Who We Are

Our history and organizational background

Initiated by Gideon Njini, Enterprise Development & Governance Facility (EDGF) was registered in July 2009.

EDGF functions as a community based charitable Learning Organization working in the process of promoting local living economies and supporting indigenous communities.

The Cameroon Anglophone Crisis which started in 2016 caused us to re-focus our services to be able to serve internally displaced persons relocated to the regional capital city of Bamenda.

In fulfillment of EDGF’s charitable objectives, we are working with our associated organization, the Accountancy Services & Audit (ASA) Group Cameroon and providing consultancy services to other community based charities.

At EDGF we serve by working to create common and thriving futures for everyone.

EDGF Theory of Change

If internally displaced persons and their children – crisis victims – are supported with education, job creation skills, and micro business development with seed capital; facilitating and enabling the vulnerable to gain access and be financially involved on issues of their livelihoods.

Back in the rural communities our services of safe drinking water projects enhanced with WASH education and facilities improves our client communities’ well-being and healthy living.

We facilitate and support our client communities to be engaged in micro-enterprise, improved farming systems and environmental protection.

Our impactful services aim at improving people and community knowledge, their incomes, wellbeing, and resilience, that will lead to empowered individuals and communities capable of self-reliance, peaceful coexistence, and sustainable development.

EDGF’s theory of change contributes directly to its vision of Social Justice, Love, Peace, and Equal Opportunity for all.

EDGF’s Community Services

Guided by our Theory of Change, we offer the following services:

1

Fee support for the children of the poor and orphans to attend school.

2

Adult literacy training.

3

Financial literacy and inclusion of the poor, especially women.

4

Water Sanitation Hygiene (WASH) and water supply projects.

5

Occupational – Job place Self-help Skills for the underprivileged.

6

Community micro enterprise creation and development.

7

Farming systems improvements / Environmental Education.

8

Services to Internally Displaced Persons (IDPs), most especially women and youths.
