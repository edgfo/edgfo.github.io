About EDGFO

Enterprise Development and Governance Facility

Who We Are

Enterprise Development and Governance Facility (EDGFO) is an organization committed to supporting enterprise development, responsible governance, innovation and sustainable community development.

Our Mission

To empower individuals, enterprises and communities through practical development initiatives, capacity building, governance support and access to opportunities.

Our Vision

A society where enterprises thrive, institutions are accountable and communities have the capacity to create sustainable opportunities for all.

Our Values

Integrity, accountability, inclusion, innovation, transparency, partnership and sustainable development guide our work.

Our Programmes & Projects

Practical initiatives designed to create lasting impact.

Enterprise Development

Supporting entrepreneurs and small businesses with knowledge, skills, capacity building and opportunities for sustainable growth.

Good Governance

Promoting accountability, transparency, participation and effective institutional leadership.

Community Development

Supporting initiatives that improve livelihoods, inclusion and sustainable development in communities.

Digital Innovation

Promoting the use of digital technologies, innovation and knowledge to create new opportunities.

Youth & Skills Development

Equipping young people with relevant skills and opportunities for education, employment and enterprise.

Partnerships

Working with communities, institutions, governments, development partners and other stakeholders to achieve shared development objectives.

Be Part of the Change

Your support, partnership and participation can help us build stronger enterprises, improve governance and create sustainable opportunities for communities.
