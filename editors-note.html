Welcome to EDGF

Welcome to the Enterprise Development and Governance Facility's digital platform.

EDGF is committed to contributing to a society where enterprises can thrive, institutions can operate with integrity and accountability, and communities can access opportunities that support sustainable development.

Through this platform, we will share information about our programmes and projects, publications, activities, opportunities and perspectives on issues relevant to enterprise development and governance.

We believe that meaningful development requires collaboration. Governments, businesses, civil society, communities, development partners, young people and individuals all have important roles to play.

We therefore invite you to explore our work, engage with our ideas, share your perspectives and join us in building practical solutions to development challenges.

Building a Culture of Enterprise and Responsible Governance

Date: To be updated

Sustainable development is not achieved through isolated interventions. It requires an environment where individuals can develop their capabilities, enterprises can grow, institutions can perform effectively and communities can participate in shaping their future.

At EDGF, our aim is to contribute to this environment by bringing together enterprise development, governance, capacity building, innovation, research and partnerships.

This section will regularly feature the Editor's reflections on emerging issues, EDGF initiatives, development trends and opportunities for collaboration.
