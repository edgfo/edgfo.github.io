Programmes & Projects

Our programmes and projects are designed to strengthen enterprises, improve governance, promote innovation and create sustainable opportunities for individuals and communities.

What We Do

Turning ideas into practical development initiatives.

EDGF develops and supports programmes that respond to enterprise, governance, community and development challenges. Our approach combines capacity building, innovation, partnerships and practical action to create lasting impact.

Our Programmes

Key areas through which EDGF delivers its development work.

Enterprise Development

Supporting entrepreneurs, start-ups and small businesses with knowledge, skills, mentorship and opportunities for sustainable growth.

Good Governance

Promoting accountability, transparency, participation, responsible leadership and effective institutional practices.

Community Development

Supporting community-led initiatives that improve livelihoods, inclusion, resilience and sustainable development.

Digital Innovation

Promoting digital skills, technology, innovation and digital solutions that expand access to information and economic opportunities.

Youth & Skills Development

Equipping young people with practical skills, entrepreneurship knowledge and opportunities for education, employment and enterprise.

Partnerships & Capacity Building

Building partnerships and strengthening the capacity of institutions, organizations and community actors to achieve sustainable development objectives.

How We Work

Our programmes are guided by a practical and participatory approach.

Needs Assessment: We identify development needs and priorities through consultation with relevant stakeholders.
Capacity Building: We provide knowledge, skills and practical support that enable individuals and institutions to achieve their goals.
Partnership: We work with communities, institutions, businesses, government and development partners.
Innovation: We encourage practical and creative solutions to development challenges.
Monitoring & Learning: We promote evidence-based learning, accountability and continuous improvement.
